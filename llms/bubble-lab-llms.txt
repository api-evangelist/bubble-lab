# Bubble Lab

> Bubble Lab is an open-core, agentic workflow automation platform built for developers. Its Apache-2.0 TypeScript engine composes AI agents and integrations as typed, composable "Bubbles" and stitches them into production-grade "BubbleFlow" pipelines with end-to-end type safety, built-in tracing, and step-level observability. Workflows are fully runnable, self-hostable, and exportable, or authored visually in the hosted Bubble Studio. Backed by Y Combinator (W26).
